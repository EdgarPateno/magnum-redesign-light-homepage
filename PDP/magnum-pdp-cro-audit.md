# Magnum Supps — Product Page CRO (Conversion Rate Optimization) Audit

**Pages audited (scraped live):**

- **Magnum Quattro** (protein) — `/products/magnum-quattro-original-cad`
- **Magnum Limitless** (pre-workout) — `/products/magnum-limitless-cad`
- **Beach Body Igniter Stack** (bundle) — `/products/beach-body-igniter-stack-cad`

---

## Executive Summary — Top 3 revenue-impacting gaps

1. **The flagship bundle is unbuyable.** The Beach Body Igniter Stack PDP (Product Detail Page) renders with **every variant flagged "sold out or unavailable" and the Add-to-Cart button `disabled`**, while still advertising a 30% discount ($179.97 → $125.98). Your highest-AOV (Average Order Value) product page converts at zero. Meanwhile 3 of 5 Quattro flavors and 1 of 4 Limitless flavors are also sold out with no waitlist/back-in-stock capture. This is pure leaked demand.

2. **Subscription is buried and defaulted off.** Magnum offers 15% subscribe-and-save, but the PDP **defaults to "One-time purchase"** and the option is a plain radio with no value framing. Every benchmark that does this well — Ghost, PVL, Transparent Labs — **pre-selects Subscribe & Save** and surrounds it with flexibility copy ("cancel or pause anytime, 30 days"). You are defaulting customers into your worst LTV (Lifetime Value) path.

3. **Trust and proof are thin vs. the category.** Quattro shows free shipping and a 4.79/43-review star block, but **no money-back guarantee, no third-party/NSF (National Sanitation Foundation) testing badge, and no per-serving cost framing.** Transparent Labs surfaces 4,965 reviews + clinically-dosed + third-party tested above the fold; 1st Phorm shows 37,340 reviews + a money-back guarantee; BPN (Bare Performance Nutrition) shows NSF + GMP (Good Manufacturing Practices) + third-party. Magnum's review counts (43, 15) are an order of magnitude behind and the trust scaffolding around them is missing.

## Section-by-Section Findings

### 1. Above the Fold
**Current State:** Quattro shows gallery (modal zoom), title, size + flavor swatch selectors, a 4.79★/43-review block near the top, quantity stepper, subscription radios (defaulting to one-time), and "Add to cart." Limitless is the same minus the size dimension.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** The bones are right — star rating ATF and swatch selectors are good. But the subscription default leaks LTV, sold-out flavor swatches dead-end the buyer, and there's no guarantee/trust microcopy near the CTA (Call to Action) to reduce purchase anxiety.

**Benchmark:** Transparent Labs pre-selects Subscribe & Save and stacks "Save 10% · Free Shipping · Cancel or pause anytime · 30 days" right at the selector.

**Recommended Action:** Default the subscription radio to Subscribe & Save with the savings and flexibility spelled out; add a one-line guarantee + free-shipping-threshold strip directly under the ATC.

### 2. Product Image Gallery
**Current State:** ~11 product images with a working zoom modal ("Open media in modal"), thumbnail rail, left/right slide. **Zero video** on any Magnum PDP.

**Verdict:** ADD (video) / KEEP (gallery mechanics)

**Why:** Supplement buyers want to see texture, mixability, and scoop size. Static images can't show "zero clumping / mixes clean," which is a top protein objection.

**Benchmark:** Transparent Labs runs 2 videos in-gallery; HD Muscle and 1st Phorm both embed product video.

**Recommended Action:** Add one 10–15s mixability/lifestyle video as gallery slide 2 on Quattro and Limitless.

### 3. Benefit / Hook Section
**Current State:** Quattro leads into "Product Features / Intended Use / Directions" — solid but **feature-first** ("4 Protein Isolate Blend, 30g protein, bromelain added"). Benefits ("zero gas or bloating," "perfect for lactose sensitivities") are buried inside the feature list.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** The strongest differentiator — gut-friendly, no bloat — is Magnum's real hook and it's hidden in a spec bullet.

**Benchmark:** PVL leads Iso Cleaar with an outcome line ("Light doesn't mean less — 20g protein, 0g sugar, 80 calories, no dairy").

**Recommended Action:** Add 3–4 outcome-led benefit icons/bullets immediately below the ATF: "Zero bloat · 30g 4-source protein · Lactose-friendly · Kosher & gluten-free."

### 4. Ingredient Transparency
**Current State:** Supplement facts appear to be served as **flat images** (13 detected), inside an accordion. No clinically-dosed callouts, no per-ingredient research notes, no third-party seal.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** Transparency is now table-stakes and a conversion lever for the informed lifter segment. An image-only facts panel isn't scannable or mobile-friendly and signals less rigor than competitors.

**Benchmark:** Transparent Labs labels doses "clinically dosed" with third-party testing; BPN shows NSF + GMP.

**Recommended Action:** Add 2–3 key-ingredient callout cards (ingredient, dose, one-line "why") above the facts image; if Magnum has any third-party/lab testing, badge it on the PDP.

### 5. Social Proof
**Current State:** Star rating + count visible ATF (good), review widget below. Counts are low (Quattro 43, Limitless 15). No UGC (User-Generated Content) photos, Q&A (Questions and Answers), or video testimonials detected.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** 15–43 reviews next to competitors' thousands is a credibility gap. Low counts also mean no photo/UGC proof, which protein buyers rely on.

**Benchmark:** 1st Phorm 37,340 reviews; Transparent Labs 4,965; BPN 4.9 across thousands.

**Recommended Action:** Run a post-purchase review-request flow with photo incentive; enable a "verified buyer" tag and photo reviews in the widget; add a Q&A block.

### 6. Upsell / Cross-Sell / Bundle
**Current State:** A cross-sell row exists at the bottom of each PDP (e.g., on Quattro: PRIMER, LIMITLESS, NEKTR with their own star ratings + ATC) — but it is **not framed as a bundle or "complete your stack,"** and there's no bundle-discount mechanic on the PDP. The dedicated Beach Body bundle is broken (see Executive Summary).

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** You have the cross-sell components but no incentive or narrative to lift AOV; an unframed "you might like" row converts far worse than an incentivized stack.

**Benchmark:** BPN "Complete Your Stack," Transparent Labs "Complete Your Stack" with ratings, Ghost "Build Your Bundle."

**Recommended Action:** Convert the cross-sell row into a "Complete Your Stack — save X%" widget with a real bundle discount applied at add-to-cart.

### 7. Sticky Add-to-Cart Bar
**Current State:** Sticky ATC exists; on mobile it shows **only "Add to cart"** — no product thumbnail, price, or selected variant.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** A bare sticky button forces the user to scroll back up to confirm flavor/price before committing.

**Benchmark:** Ghost/Transparent Labs sticky bars carry product name, price, and variant.

**Recommended Action:** Add thumbnail + price + selected flavor to the sticky bar.

### 8. Subscription / Recurring Revenue
**Current State:** 15% off via deliver-every-week/2-weeks/2-months, but **defaults to one-time** and the savings/flexibility aren't sold. Subscription is absent on the bundle PDP.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** Defaulting off and under-selling the offer suppresses subscription take-rate — the single biggest LTV lever for a consumable.

**Benchmark:** Ghost, PVL, Transparent Labs all default to Subscribe & Save with explicit "cancel/pause anytime" copy.

**Recommended Action:** Pre-select Subscribe & Save; add "$X.XX/order · cancel or pause anytime · skip any shipment" under the radio.

### 9. Trust & Risk Reversal
**Current State:** Free shipping referenced; **no money-back guarantee, no certifications badge, no per-serving cost.**

**Verdict:** ADD

**Why:** Removing purchase risk is one of the cheapest conversion lifts available, and you're the only audited brand showing none of guarantee / third-party / per-serving.

**Benchmark:** 1st Phorm (money-back), BPN (NSF/GMP/3rd-party), Transparent Labs (satisfaction guarantee + clinically dosed).

**Recommended Action:** Add a guarantee + free-shipping-threshold + (if applicable) testing badge trust strip under the ATC, and an "as low as $X/serving" line at the price.

### 10. Educational / Content Section
**Current State:** Accordions for Features / Intended Use / Directions / Ingredients / Reviews. Functional but light on "why it works."

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** Magnum positions premium; the PDP doesn't carry premium-level education to justify the $69.99 price vs. cheaper whey.

**Benchmark:** 1st Phorm tabs Description / Ingredients / How To Use / What To Avoid / FAQ / Research; HD Muscle leads with research.

**Recommended Action:** Add a short "Why 4-source protein digests better" explainer and a product-specific FAQ (mixability, lactose, when to take).

### 11. Mobile PDP
**Current State:** Sticky ATC present (button-only), swatch selectors work, content in accordions. Page feels reasonably fast.

**Verdict:** IMPROVE

**Why:** Sticky bar lacks price/variant; sold-out swatches still occupy the thumb zone without a back-in-stock path.

**Recommended Action:** Enrich sticky bar; add "Notify me" on sold-out swatches.

## Feature Gap Analysis

- **Subscribe & Save defaulted ON** — Adopt — every strong competitor does it; biggest LTV lever
- **Money-back / satisfaction guarantee on PDP** — Adopt — TL & 1st Phorm show it; you show nothing
- **Third-party / NSF / GMP testing badge** — Evaluate — adopt if Magnum has testing; BPN's clearest differentiator
- **Per-serving cost framing** — Adopt — cheap anxiety-reducer at the price point
- **Bundle discount mechanic on PDP** — Adopt — convert the existing cross-sell row into an incentivized stack
- **In-gallery product video** — Adopt — TL/HD Muscle/1st Phorm all have it; you have none
- **Back-in-stock / waitlist on sold-out variants** — Adopt — you're losing demand on 3/5 Quattro flavors today
- **Photo/UGC + Q&A in reviews** — Adopt — closes the credibility gap on low review counts
- **Sticky ATC with price + variant** — Adopt — yours is button-only
- **Structured (non-image) supplement facts + ingredient callouts** — Evaluate — improves scannability & transparency signal
- **"What to avoid" / deeper education** — Evaluate — 1st Phorm/HD Muscle differentiate here; medium effort
